# CYNTHERA — SAFE IMPLEMENTATION OF CLINICALTRIALS.GOV + SHARED DISEASE RELATION FIXES
## IMPLEMENTATION PHASE — AFTER FORENSIC AUDIT
## DO NOT CHANGE DECISION WEIGHTS OR BENCHMARK LABELS

You have completed the forensic ClinicalTrials.gov audit.

The audit identified multiple confirmed causes of valid clinical-negative evidence being lost:

1. ClinicalTrials.gov result retrieval is hard-truncated to `studies[:20]`.
2. Drug matching incorrectly treats `"Nivolumab Placebo"` as actual Nivolumab.
3. Combination/background therapy attribution fails to recognize constant co-medications across arms.
4. `whyStopped` keyword parsing can misclassify `"no safety concern"` as safety.
5. Disease matching relies on naive token overlap:
   - `"stroke"` can match `"hemorrhagic stroke"` and cause false approval inheritance.
   - `"Traumatic brain injury"` fails to match `"Subdural Hematoma, Chronic"` and can discard a genuine trial.
6. ClinicalTrials.gov structured results can be present without sufficient statistical evidence, so `hasResults=True` must NOT automatically become negative evidence.
7. Statistical interpretation has a known superiority/non-inferiority risk and MUST NOT be broadened in this implementation.

Your job now is to implement the smallest scientifically safe fixes.
