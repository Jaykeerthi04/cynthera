8. METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This evaluation uses a hold-out benchmark of 30 drug-disease pairs
  that are completely disjoint from the 25-case regression benchmark.
  Labels are evaluation-only ground truth and are never passed to the
  reasoning engine. The production pipeline receives only (drug, disease)
  and returns a recommendation independently of any benchmark label.

  Dual scoring addresses a fundamental epistemic question:
  Should 'no evidence found' count as a correct negative or as uncertainty?

  Standard scoring (UNKNOWN = NEGATIVE): Penalizes the system for not
  detecting that a pair is therapeutically invalid. This is the harsher metric.

  Epistemic scoring (UNKNOWN != NEGATIVE): Rewards the system for correctly
  expressing uncertainty about pairs where evidence simply does not exist.
  This reflects CYNTHERA's core epistemic principle: absence of evidence
  is not evidence of absence.
